Enable the camera first. Then click Arm capture while beside the laptop, step away into your signing position, and wait. The app reserves 4 seconds for you to move, then starts the countdown automatically when pose and at least one hand are visible in 4 of 5 samples. You do not need to click again; the button becomes Cancel wait while armed. During capture, the separate live landmark preview pauses to reduce CPU competition. You may still stop manually when guided timed capture is disabled. The classifier receives each unique browser video frame at about 25 FPS where supported; actual frame timing is reported after capture.
